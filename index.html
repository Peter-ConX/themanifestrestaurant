What Guests Say

The Experience
in Their Words.

A
Amir Asghari Local Guide · 27 reviews
★★★★★

"From the snacks — the 'still warm' salt and vinegar crisps were wonderful: a large bowl of freshly cut, freshly cooked crisps, unlike anything I have experienced elsewhere. Amazingly, the price was comparable to chips from a fast-food place.

From the small plates, the baby leek, Blacksticks Blue velouté and sage was exceptional. If there is a heaven where food is served, this should be on the menu.

From the large plates, the duck was a revelation... perhaps what I had previously eaten was fake duck, if this was what duck can really be.

Overall, a rather expensive, but deeply satisfactory and unforgettable experience."
